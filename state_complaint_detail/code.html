Severe Water Logging & Pipeline Burst

Major water pipeline rupture causing severe flooding in residential areas, affecting structural integrity of multiple homes and disrupting daily life.

description Complaint Details

Residents of Banjara Hills Road No. 12 have been experiencing severe water logging since yesterday evening due to a massive underground pipeline burst. The water pressure is extremely high, causing structural damage to the perimeter walls of three independent houses. The flooded street is completely unnavigable for vehicles and pedestrians.

Despite temporary sandbagging by local residents, the water level continues to rise. We suspect the main supply line from the Krishna drinking water project has been compromised. Immediate intervention by HMWS&SB is required to shut off the main valve and initiate emergency repairs before further property damage occurs.
